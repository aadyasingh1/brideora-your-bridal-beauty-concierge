Brideora
AI-Powered Hyperlocal Bridal Beauty Marketplace for Delhi

1. Problem Statement
Planning bridal beauty services in Delhi is fragmented, time-consuming, and stressful.
Brides often have to search across Instagram pages, WhatsApp groups, Google listings, wedding websites, and personal recommendations to find suitable makeup artists, salons, hair stylists, and mehendi artists.
Current platforms have several limitations:
Bridal beauty services are scattered across multiple platforms.
Many artists do not display transparent pricing.
Brides struggle to find services within their budget.
Home-service providers are difficult to discover.
Existing platforms are not hyperlocal enough.
Users spend hours comparing artists and packages.
There is little personalization in recommendations.
Booking and communication often happen manually through calls and WhatsApp.
As a result, brides face decision fatigue during one of the most important events of their lives.

2. Our Solution
Brideora is an AI-powered hyperlocal bridal beauty marketplace focused exclusively on Delhi.
The platform helps brides discover, compare, and book bridal beauty services based on:
Budget
Location
Wedding function
Service type
Home service availability
Personal preferences
Brideora combines marketplace functionality with AI-powered recommendations to help users find the perfect beauty professional quickly and confidently.

3. Vision
To become Delhi's most trusted bridal beauty discovery and booking platform by making beauty services accessible, transparent, personalized, and convenient.

9. Business Model & Revenue Streams
Brideora follows a marketplace business model that generates revenue by connecting brides with verified beauty professionals and salons.
The platform creates value for both customers and service providers by offering discovery, booking, visibility, and personalized recommendations.
9.1 Commission on Bookings (Primary Revenue Stream)
Brideora earns a commission on every successful booking made through the platform.
Example:
Bridal Makeup Package: ₹15,000
Brideora Commission (10%): ₹1,500
Artist Receives: ₹13,500
This performance-based model ensures that service providers only pay when they receive actual business.
Benefits
No upfront cost for customers
Low risk for artists
Scalable revenue model
Encourages platform adoption

9.2 Featured Listings & Sponsored Placement
Artists and salons can pay to receive higher visibility on the platform.
Featured professionals appear:
At the top of search results
On the homepage

In AI-generated recommendations
Example Plans:
Featured Listing: ₹999/month
Premium Featured Listing: ₹1,999/month
Benefits
